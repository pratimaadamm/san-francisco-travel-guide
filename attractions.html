Tips for First-Time Visitors

Best Time to Visit

Late spring and early fall bring clear skies and comfortable temperatures. Arrive just before sunset to watch the bridge shift from warm golden light to night-time sparkle.

Photography Tips

For iconic photos, try Battery Spencer for a sweeping front view, or Marshall’s Beach for dramatic shoreline angles. Foggy mornings create dreamy pastel scenes; golden hour brings rich orange reflections on the water.

Getting Around

Parking can be limited on busy days. Consider using Muni, rideshare, or renting a bike to cross the bridge and continue into Sausalito for a relaxed waterfront stroll.

Local Secrets

Visit just after a light rain for crystal-clear views of the skyline. On cooler evenings, bring layers—the wind over the bay can feel colder than the city streets below.
